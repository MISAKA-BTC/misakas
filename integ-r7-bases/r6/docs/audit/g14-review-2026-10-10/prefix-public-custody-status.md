# Prospective public prefix custody

This contract is defined only for a claim accepted under the independent staged-public-court fence, from an accepted signed FP prefix job and an immutable registered prefix-state relation. It preserves ADR-0177 D7: a verifier supplies its own correctly held model/tokenizer/configuration. Public prefix source obligations concern the claim's original authenticated execution leaves, not model publication.

The producer's signed commitment covers `fp_claim_id_v3`, including the job and execution root. At acceptance the rooted custody contract retains that execution root, exact job identity, registered CU ruleset identity, acceptance clock and hash of the original staged terms. Later paid clock extensions do not rewrite this original identity.

Under this prospective contract a signed `Valid` receipt accepts custody of the claim-specific original authenticated prefix sources. The accepted receipt is qualified only when its actual seat credit is rooted, its claim and signed clock match, and the signer is an actual bound seat. The contract saves a digest of the exact accepted signed receipt together with root/job/ruleset/original terms. `Sampled`, mere membership, pre-fence receipts, foreign claims and absent/refused receipts do not qualify. Qualification survives ordinary receipt/duty pruning and reverts through the existing staged contract journal.

Joint default demands require the producer's qualified accepted FP job and every seat of the latest actual bound Panel qualified under these rules. Missing qualification permits neither joint-default guilt nor a `Final` relying on this route. At the ordinary final deadline such an unqualified claim expires as `PanelUnavailable`, with no invented DA slash; existing pending accusations retain their ordinary continuation. It earns no Final/model activation credit. This creates a deliberate liveness limitation: a refusing seat can prevent this qualification and Final, so this route does not prove objective conviction of an unsigned refusing seat.

The public-source attempt engine isolates one paid attempt per actual custodian plus the literal owner. It verifies the original step opening and whole legacy leaf hash before exposing any bytes. An invalid or stalled answer cannot alter the owner's semantic continuation or consume another custodian's slot. Individual invalid answers are not convictions. Joint default is the absence of any complete authenticated answer at the literal rooted deadline, against the saved signed joint obligation.

The attempt plan prices all candidate capacities, bounded signed carriers, every saved candidate consistency hash, rollback/storage bytes and reorg work. Outer demand admission must add these quantities to paid work and establish material and all participant holds before creating the pending row. The pure engine alone is not a qualified transport, demand fold, network service, default collector or activation theorem; those integrations and maximal-profile evidence remain required.

Historic unqualified claims and dormant shipping fences retain their old semantics. No legacy scope/session cap is silently relaxed.
