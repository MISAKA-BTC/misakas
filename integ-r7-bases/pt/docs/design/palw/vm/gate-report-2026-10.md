## 概要(日本語)

- **ゲートは閉じている**(RFC-0005 §I.1.4)。VM に帰せられるギャップ `cov_BVM − cov_AOT` は 0.0 ポイント(閾値 3)。コーパスに制御フローを宣言する config は 1 本もなく(M1 131 本中 NEEDS_CONTROL 0 本)、RFC-0002 が登録できない約 36 % は形式・importer・lowerer・primitive のギャップで、VM では 1 つも閉じない(GB2)。
- **Part I の BVM は作らない**(RFC の Decision:「ゲートが開く場合のみ」・「Part I と rung B の両方は作らない」)。
- NEEDS_VM になるのは C13(ルーター/カスケード、ワークフロー=Part II の判断。ガード付き TIR で閉じられる)と、仮想的な C12(数千ラウンドのループ)だけで、どちらも使用実績のあるチェックポイント群はない。
- **測っていないもの**: M3(ダウンロード数・`architectures[0]` 別件数)はハブへのネットワーク接続が必要で承認されていないため **測定不能**として、RFC §I.1.5 と hf-coverage §2 の値を使う。M2・M4 は実在チェックポイントではなくパス統計の**モデル**。

- **C9 early exit (LayerSkip-shaped, 8B decoder)**: Model: 32 layers; the share of tokens leaving after layer e is a half-geometric profile with the last layer taking the remainder (E[e] ≈ 18). The expansion is the full model, so ρ_w = 1 and ρ_a = L / E[e]. Illustrative: no public checkpoint is measured.

- **C10 mixture-of-depths (capacity 50 %, every block gated)**: Model: capacity 0.5 on every block — a `Select` per block and a masked history row for a skipped position. ρ_a ≈ 1 / capacity = 2. Illustrative.

- **C11 dynamic expert count (Mixtral-shaped, k_max 2)**: Model: tokens use 1 or 2 experts (60 % / 40 %); expert MACs are 92 % of the base and are paid at k_max by the expansion. Illustrative.

- **C12 halting / looped depth (N = 32 rounds of a small block)**: Model: the base is one round (a tiny block); the loop runs up to 32 rounds, halting after a mean of 8. The repeated block is one schedule entry per round with global params, so the schedule (≤ 1,024 layers) bounds N·layers. Illustrative.

- **C12 halting at thousands of rounds (N = 2048)**: Model: the same 2-layer round run up to 2,048 times — 4,096 layer occurrences, past the schedule's 1,024, so the expansion cannot be written while the longest path's dynamic cost (≈ 6.9·10^10 MACs) still fits the per-position ceiling and the loop-folded program is one round. RFC-0005 §I.1.6: the one C12 case that is NEEDS_VM (`N` in the thousands). Illustrative.

- **C13 router over two small LMs (the D-B4 shape)**: RFC-0005 §Activation D-B4: a router over two small language models. The expansion runs both arms at every position (ρ_w ≈ 2, ρ_a from the 70/30 split).

- **C13 cascade over four 7–8B models**: Model: a dispatcher over four 3–8B decoders (Llama-3.1, Qwen2.5-7B, Mistral-7B, SmolLM3-3B) with frequencies 40/30/20/10. The sum of the arms' MACs is compared with the per-position ceiling, the longest path's with the same.

- **C13 router over large arms (Mixtral, Qwen3-30B-A3B, Phi-4, Llama-3.1-8B)**: Model: a router over arms that are individually near the per-position MACs ceiling; whether the sum breaks it while the longest path does not is read from admission.

- **C14 adaptive steps (up to 64 steps)**: Model: a job runs up to 64 steps (positions), a mean of 24. Steps are positions (RFC-0003); the expansion fixes the step count. Illustrative.

- **router: small/large by difficulty**: A difficulty classifier sends 80 % of tasks to a 0.5B model and 20 % to a 7B model.

- **cascade: try small, escalate**: A 1.5B model answers; a verifier escalates 30 % of tasks to an 8B model, 5 % further to a 30B-A3B MoE (mean 1.35 calls).

- **tool planner over three specialists**: A planner picks one of a code model, a general model and an extraction model per step; up to 8 steps, a mean of 3.

- **agent loop (plan, act, critique)**: One 8B model called in a bounded loop (at most 16 rounds, mean 5) with a 0.5B critic: the loop is data-dependent control around whole-model calls.

## What follows

1. **Gate closed ⇒ no BVM.** RFC-0005 Part I's language, admission, step space and Control-flow Court are not built; the Decision section of the RFC records it ("only if the gate opens", "never both Part I and rung B").
2. The verdicts that name a real cost of control are `EXPANDABLE` (waste `ρ`, TIR as it is) and, for a router over whole models, `NEEDS_VM(max_macs_per_position)` — which guarded TIR closes while the unrolled guard DAG fits the static ceilings. That is a workflow pattern; if it proves a demand, the route is Part II's rung B, not a bounded layer.
3. Re-measure at every prim-set revision, at every lowerer release that closes a parser gap, and when a family with data-dependent control reaches 1 % of new checkpoints in a quarter (§I.1.4); M3 needs the operator's approval for hub access.
