## Limits

It is synthetic state, with an open-court counter; it is not an ordinary admission, real service, signed receipt ingress or block-fold proof. No producer execution capture or secret state is passed to the predicate.

The node still needs to generate this proof and obtain its position-specific public openings. The 100,000-byte direct evidence ceiling is unchanged: large admitted version-6 jobs can exceed it before the rest of the proof is added, so bounded multipart terminal carriage remains necessary.
