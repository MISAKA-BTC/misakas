# Commitment-only kernel responses — implementation checkpoint

The independently versioned `palw_court_commitment_scope_v1` fence is absent on shipping networks. For program/pipeline claims committed at or after it, node values that depend on registered model parameters are omitted from position responses; public and model-independent claim material remains owed. The classifier rejects a clear value where omission is required. A demand is not refused because another requester used a global disclosure allowance. Historical claims retain their original response contract.

Under ADR-0177 D7, the outsider uses its registered model and authenticated commitments to replay computation and decode after checking the owed DA material. A first mismatch is filed through the existing objective court. Incorrect verifier model acquisition returns an error rather than a producer conviction. Silence on an owed position still follows the existing DA-default and producer-sanction path.

The consumer records the activation in kernel-route auxiliary table 46. The auxiliary root authenticates the response contract. Public route reads inject it into both cached and reconstructed ledgers; the ledger itself keeps its historical row/root encoding. Reverting the auxiliary row removes the policy even when a cache exists. The new route snapshot/cache test is source-written but not yet executed. The scope does not close the large segmented-value hiding-leaf court or all-family/resource/inclusion gaps.
