# G14 Acceptance Ledger

Scope is the user's full property, not just the new PublicPrefix lane. A producer
and every Panel signer may collude. One ordinary bonded outside verifier must
reach the objectively correct outcome for every violation covered by the active
plan using public authenticated claim material and its own registered model.
Existing allowed profiles, legacy routes, kernel routes and their maximum sizes
remain in scope. This ledger does not arm dormant fences or change reward policy.

`PASS` below means the particular evidence supports the stated requirement at
the stated level. A build, resource quote or fold test cannot establish a service
terminal. Failed/running/filtered-out tests and later source changes do not inherit
PASS. Historical family matrices are evidence history, not current completion.
