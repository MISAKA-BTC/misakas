## C. Rulings needed (not values)

1. **R_total composition for attempt / receipt blocks.** The uncarved worker base (`worker_base − carve`; 0 at testnet-12's
   720 ‰ carve when the base is 72 %) and the block's tx-fee worker shares are paid per entitled block, including an attempt block
   whose attempt the budget refused. Counting them in R needs a coinbase change (withhold the base of an attempt-lane block whose
   attempt is refused). Lead default: outside R in v1 (with market/Round fees, per the 2026-10-11 ruling), bounded by B.
2. **Withdrawal hold of a Panel=1-only bond.** The long (7-day) view is the bond's withdrawal hold, so a bond that only ever made
   Panel=1 claims still waits 7 days to exit. Shortening it to 1 day would let exit → re-registration → Panel=0 use up to six more
   days of rate. Lead default: 7 days (safe).
3. **Per-day rates for Round rights and monetary income rows.** They are bounded by the 7-day long view only (no day view).
4. **P_m single application.** With the V3 allocation, per-model reward-bearing block rights carry P_m; the existing coinbase R
   clip (`palw_model_available_v1`, coinbase-derived origins only since RFC-0016 P4) applies P_m again. Lead default: under V3 the
   R clip does not apply to claims already admitted under the model's block rights. The implemented block rights are RFC-0016's
   reward-right level (the physical attempt block still exists when its claim is refused); RFC-0016 D-Q1's physical layer (V+,
   Draft) is not implemented.
4b. **Per-claim ceiling vs user-paid escrow.** Formula v2 requires a rights-slicing budget policy; RFC-0016 P4 refuses a kernel
   claim whose job escrow exceeds the per-claim ceiling `r_c` (`EscrowNotReservable`). Whether the per-claim ceiling applies to
   user-paid escrow at all (it splits COINBASE period rights) is a ruling.
5. **Verifier capital definition for Panel=0.** Implemented as Panel seat locks behind live claims (`seat_exposure`); Panel=0
   has no seats, so OPV verifier capital counts nothing until the verifier participation / liability / hold / reward rules (and
   bond-without-checking countermeasures) are defined (user: required before activation).
6. **ADR-0166 §6 standby seats** (phase 2) and **ADR-0173 §7** six open questions; **ADR-0178 §11** (`W_p`, heights, L2FC).
7. **EVM `nativeCurrency` display** (ADR-0174): kept `MSK` / `MSK` in the wallet add-chain entry for compatibility; switching it
   to `Misaka` / `BILI` changes what existing wallet entries show.
