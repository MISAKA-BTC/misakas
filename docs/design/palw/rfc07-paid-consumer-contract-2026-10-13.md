# RFC07 paid consumer and lifetime contract — 2026-10-13

## Implementation boundary

Native paid source admission currently connects **ModelConformance only**. Its target is the accepted immutable conformance attempt, its sponsor is that commitment's authenticated signer, and its finite retention horizon is derived from the source Final/evidence/binding windows. `PalwBeaconSourcePolicyV1::target`, `beacon_source_inputs_v4`, `beacon_source_fee_inputs_v1`, and the onboarding SDK require this authority. The Panel v4/v5 verifier and frozen `PalwPanelSourceSnapshotV1` do not supply a native sponsor/funding permit or create an admissible source target. A claim-verification target also has no such adapter. A pure beacon verifier is not a paid consumer connection.

The existing common money path is reusable: source/fee digests are signed, funding debits actual native free collateral into a separately rooted escrow, each paid opt-in reserves the recipient's fixed R entitlement before its source rows are accepted, and the closing tick queues that same entitlement on payment or cancels it on principal refund. The source subject is immutable `(job, seal)` until reveal, with context and recipient in the income-row ID. It creates no second computation claim, execution ticket or Round right. Kernel table 30 owns the separate source collateral; table 27 kinds 5/6 own funding/promises; native budget rows and pending UTXO payouts retain the actual payment liability.

The following contract is required before connecting the other targets. It is a **design requirement, not an implemented new activation or a payment API**. Historical v3/v4/v5 hashes, source terms, attempt rows, and stored contracts retain their codecs. New target authority rows need a versioned schema and a separately reviewed dormant fence; assigning their table/tag numbers is part of the implementation inventory.

## One common authenticated authority

A versioned paid-target authority must bind the full source `BeaconContextV1` and finite `BeaconSourceTermsV1`, consumer kind, canonical subject row and its accepted block/DAA, frozen source eligibility/exclusions, the exact sponsor bond and payout identity, sponsor permit digest, cost-review digest, minimum fee, capacity, and each liability/material deadline. It must bind a pre-entropy context, not a caller-supplied seed, current-tip eligibility, arbitrary candidate, or a proof encoded by the proposer. A single consumer validator should derive this authority from rooted state and return the same source/fee admission inputs used by the common ledger and native budget path.

A sponsor permit is an authenticated native signed object authorizing **its own** free collateral for one exact context, fixed gross fee, capacity, expiry and consumer budget scope. It cannot authorize issuance, another payer's bond, an unfunded job escrow, or reallocation of an EXEC root's existing signed budget. For Panel/claim targets, the consumer freezes one canonical valid permit before sources become admissible; a missing or expired permit leaves the target unavailable. Selecting/replacing a payer or fee contract after entropy is visible is refused. A caller-supplied sponsorship field is insufficient. Multiple sponsors require a separately specified conservation/allocation rule; this contract authorizes no implicit pooling.

## Admission and money closure

The consumer must run these checks through one transactional transition, using the same rooted generation:

1. Derive and freeze the canonical target/authority; check its sponsor permit, pre-entropy position, horizon, policy and global/per-producer source caps. Freeze the exact cost tuple and gross floor, including material/court/capital costs for that horizon. Existing fixture prices do not establish measured costs.
2. Fund the exact signed contract with `FundBeaconSourceFee`. Use the existing strict native collateral debit and funding row. For v5 require capacity at least k, one funding per context, lawful free collateral, and exact sponsor/floor/cap. A sponsor's later default cannot consume the separated escrow. Funding is principal transfer, not source income.
3. For `OptInPaidBeaconSource`, require both immutable signed digests, the fresh ordinary seal/job/payer, eligible independent source work and finite overlapping reveal windows. Each target gets its own separately reserved source collateral. Reserve the same recipient's fixed R fee before accepting any source, promise or native money write. A duplicate/context switch/reseal cannot reuse collateral or a consumed promise. Refusal rolls back initial budget creation too.
4. Reveal retains source collateral and the source's full claim/material liability. Source withholding, reseal, deadline failure or public conviction creates a permanent veto and forfeits only its contracted collateral. Fee principal returns to the authenticated sponsor once; refund cancels the corresponding source R entitlement and earns no source income. A target veto cannot confiscate an honest source's fulfilled prepaid fee.
5. A standing timely source Final alone pays nothing. After the entire signed horizon, the common tick consumes its once-only source promise and moves the **same** R reservation into the actual payout queue before routing prepaid escrow into native pending UTXO payouts. R persists through actual payment + W, including sparse ticks and payout delay. No alternate consumer may bypass these steps with a raw settlement instruction.
6. Every source, authority, funding/promise, budget, liability/material link and payout queue mutation participates in the existing native delta journal. Restart/replay/reorg must restore one coherent generation. Budget activation migration seeds pending source promises and recently paid/queued promises with their actual payment clock; changing target type does not grandfather unbounded fees. Reapplying the accepted carrier or restoring either branch never pays twice.

The consumer's resource plan must reserve source seal/reveal/Final and dependent court/carrier inclusion capacity before admitting the fee. A mathematical overlap of reveal windows does not prove that every contracted source fits. Bounded source-slot ceilings do not prove 100k throughput. No adapter may invent a positive censorship/detection floor when public material or carriers are unavailable. Pruned joining nodes require the same authenticated authority, complete source/failure/fee inventory, material commitments and journals; dropping historical failures or failed promises to fit a snapshot is forbidden. Bounded history indexes and retirement proofs need their own acceptance evidence.

SDK/CLI/RPC APIs for each target must use the same generic authenticated authority verifier and rooted ledger/aux inventory. Source facts, fee receipts, actual native queued/paid amount and measured-cost provenance must remain distinct. Matching self-consistent RPC roots require authentication against chain history. A nonzero cost/review digest is not independent review, proof of economic independence, an actual paid UTXO, or a minimum net-profit observation.

## Authenticated finite lifetime soundness

The composed registry evaluator's counts are declarations. A live contract must additionally bind a finite review scope: chain/ruleset, the complete authorized current **and historical** suite/policy set, all relevant subject kinds, start/end positions, total adaptive-subject ceiling, retries per subject, staged-beacon ceiling, fork/offline grinding bound, coverage/source/binding assumptions, and the global error allocation. Every subject admission spends a canonical counter before entropy; the counter and subject identity survive retries, policy changes, restart and historical-row retirement inside that scope. Admission refuses exhaustion, unknown history or an unsupported policy; upgrades carry prior authorized failure terms into the cumulative union. A new review digest does not reset earlier failure probabilities.

Canonical counters must reorg with canonical state to remain deterministic. Such counters alone cannot count entropy choices on abandoned forks or offline nonce grinding. The review must separately bound those strategy-tree opportunities and charge them in G; a node-local non-rollback counter is not consensus authority. A finite counter proves no infinite deployment guarantee. Release acceptance must demonstrate counter authentication/enforcement, finite expiry/sunset behavior and a reviewed global union across the whole declared horizon. If any fork/source/censorship/horizon bound is unknown, effective approval remains zero regardless of algebraic surplus.

This authenticated lifetime contract, its consensus admission counter/schema/fence, and its Panel/Claim sponsor adapters remain **unimplemented**. Both approval registries remain empty. No independent 128-bit review or economic/source guarantee is supplied by this document.
